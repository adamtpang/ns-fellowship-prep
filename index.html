~4,000 people apply per cohort for 128 spots. This checklist won't guarantee a seat, it just makes sure you're not losing ground on the parts that are entirely in your control.
Stage 1 — The Form
Online application. First cut. Most applicants lose here on vagueness, not lack of talent.
Stage 2 — The Zoom Interview
Live conversation. They're checking if the form was true and if you're worth a room.
Stage 3 — In-Person Exam (Singapore)
You show up physically. This filters out people who were only good on a screen.
Stage 4 — The Decision
Out of your hands by this point. Worth thinking through anyway.
Structure sourced from public info on the NS Fellowship process (online form, Zoom interview, in-person exam, decision), not from Network School itself. Progress saves in your browser only, nothing is sent anywhere.
